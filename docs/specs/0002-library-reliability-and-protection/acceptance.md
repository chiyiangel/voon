可用[只读验收工具](../../../crates/voon-core/examples/reliability_scan.rs)复现扫描与强读。先明确新的 20 张样本及完整前置清单，在本机建立临时链接根，然后运行：

```sh

  /absolute/local/index.sqlite3 /absolute/selected-20-root /absolute/local/report.json
```
